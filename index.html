Sobre o eventoAbout

Da célula à rede elétricaFrom the cell to the grid

O Bat2Grid reúne pesquisadores, estudantes de pós-graduação e profissionais do setor elétrico para discutir o armazenamento de energia em baterias e sua integração à rede: da modelagem e estimação de estados das células à eletrônica de potência, às microrredes e à viabilidade econômica de sistemas BESS.

Em sua segunda edição, o evento acontece na Universidade Federal de Viçosa, com minicursos, palestras magnas de pesquisadores do Brasil e do exterior e sessões de apresentação de trabalhos em pôster e oral.

Bat2Grid brings together researchers, graduate students and power sector professionals to discuss battery energy storage and its integration into the grid: from cell modelling and state estimation to power electronics, microgrids and the economic feasibility of BESS.

The second edition takes place at the Federal University of Viçosa, with short courses, keynote talks by researchers from Brazil and abroad, and poster and oral paper sessions.

    Minicurso 1Short course 1

    Filtro de Kalman aplicado à estimação do Estado de Carga (SoC) e do Estado de Saúde (SoH) de baterias em sistemas de armazenamento de energia

    Diuary Gonçalves
    ResumoAbstract (Portuguese)

    Este minicurso apresenta o Filtro de Kalman como ferramenta para estimar o Estado de Carga (SoC) e o Estado de Saúde (SoH) de baterias, grandezas essenciais para a operação segura, eficiente e duradoura de sistemas de armazenamento conectados à rede elétrica.

    A atividade parte da importância do monitoramento de baterias em sistemas BESS, veículos elétricos e microrredes e discute as limitações de métodos tradicionais, como a contagem de Coulomb e a relação tensão de circuito aberto–SoC. Em seguida, apresenta a modelagem de baterias por circuito elétrico equivalente e a formulação do problema em espaço de estados.

    São trabalhados os fundamentos do Filtro de Kalman e de suas extensões não lineares, como o Filtro de Kalman Estendido (EKF) e o Unscented (UKF), com foco no ajuste das matrizes de covariância e na robustez frente a ruídos e incertezas do modelo. O minicurso também aborda a estimação do SoH, considerando a perda de capacidade e o aumento da resistência interna ao longo da vida útil.

    Exemplos práticos e simulações conectam a teoria à implementação em sistemas de gerenciamento de baterias (BMS).

    Minicurso 2Short course 2

    Inteligência Artificial aplicada à estimação de estados e ao balanceamento de células em baterias para sistemas de armazenamento de energia

    Ediward Amaral Carelli
    ResumoAbstract (Portuguese)

    Este minicurso apresenta técnicas de Inteligência Artificial aplicadas a dois desafios centrais do gerenciamento de baterias: a estimação de estados e o balanceamento de células, ambos decisivos para o desempenho, a segurança e a vida útil de sistemas de armazenamento integrados à rede elétrica.

    Na primeira parte, são abordadas abordagens orientadas a dados para estimar o Estado de Carga (SoC), o Estado de Saúde (SoH) e a vida útil remanescente (RUL), com redes neurais, redes recorrentes (LSTM/GRU) e outros modelos de aprendizado de máquina. São discutidos a preparação de dados de ciclagem, a seleção de variáveis, o treinamento, a validação e a avaliação dos modelos.

    Na segunda parte, o minicurso trata do balanceamento de células: por que os desequilíbrios surgem, as diferenças entre estratégias passivas e ativas e como a IA pode tornar o balanceamento mais adaptativo, aumentando a capacidade útil do pack e reduzindo a degradação.

    Palestra magna 1Keynote 1

    AI-Driven Battery Monitoring: How Artificial Intelligence Is Reshaping State Estimation, Health Prognostics and Reliability in Energy Storage Systems

    Prof. Remus Teodorescu · Aalborg University, DinamarcaDenmark
    Resumo (em inglês)Abstract

    Prof. Remus Teodorescu discusses how Artificial Intelligence is transforming the way batteries are monitored, managed and maintained in modern energy storage systems. Conventional battery management systems rely mainly on physics-based models and limited onboard measurements, which often struggle to capture non-linear behaviour, temperature effects and long-term degradation.

    The talk explores how data-driven and hybrid approaches can improve SoC and SoH estimation, Remaining Useful Life prediction and early fault detection. It also addresses cloud-connected and digital twin architectures, in which operational data continuously update battery models and support predictive maintenance.

    Finally, Prof. Teodorescu shares perspectives on the next generation of intelligent battery systems and on challenges such as data availability, model generalisation and real-time implementation.

    Palestra magna 2Keynote 2

    Microgrids and the Role of Energy Storage: Challenges and Opportunities for Reliable, Flexible and Sustainable Power Systems

    Prof. João Marcus Soares Callegari
    Resumo (em inglês)Abstract

    Prof. João Marcus Soares Callegari discusses microgrids and the central role of battery energy storage in their reliable and efficient operation, both connected to the main grid and in islanded mode.

    The talk covers microgrid architectures, operating modes and control hierarchies, and how storage contributes to power balancing, frequency and voltage support, renewable smoothing and seamless transitions between operating modes. It then addresses the main challenges: sizing and integration of battery systems, grid-forming and grid-following converter control, energy management, the impact of operation on battery lifetime, protection and economic viability.

    A Web-Based Battery Management System (BMS) Software Developed at GESEP: Characteristic Curves, Charging and Discharging Methods, and Cell Balancing Strategies

    Paulo Henrique Souza Ferreira · GESEP
    Resumo (em inglês)Abstract

    Paulo Henrique Souza Ferreira presents the BMS software developed at GESEP, which runs entirely on a web platform and makes the analysis of battery behaviour more accessible and interactive, with no local installation.

    The talk covers the tool's three main features: battery characteristic curves, charging and discharging methods such as CC and CC-CV, and cell balancing strategies. It also discusses the software architecture, its use in teaching and research, and planned improvements.

    Rede GESEPGESEP network

    GESEP Research Network: Research Lines, Ongoing Projects and Contributions from the Viçosa, Belo Horizonte and Juiz de Fora Centers

    Resumo (em inglês)Abstract

    This talk opens the second day of talks with an overview of GESEP and its research centers in Viçosa, Belo Horizonte and Juiz de Fora. It presents the history and structure of each center, the group's main research lines in battery modelling and management, power converters, microgrids and grid integration, and the research and development projects currently under way, together with opportunities for students and partners to get involved.

  • 09:00–09:30
    INCT-EPTech

    INCT-EPTech: Power Electronics Technologies for a Sustainable Energy Transition

    Prof. Telles Brunelli Lazzarin · UFSC
    Resumo (em inglês)Abstract

    Prof. Telles Brunelli Lazzarin presents the National Institute of Science and Technology in Power Electronics (INCT-EPTech), a network of around 70 researchers from 16 national and international institutions working with industrial partners.

    The talk introduces the institute's eight strategic verticals: wide-bandgap devices (SiC/GaN), efficient data centers, energy transition, energy storage, electric vehicles and charging infrastructure, human resources training, scientific entrepreneurship and artificial intelligence, with particular attention to storage and grid integration.

  • 09:30–10:30
    Palestra magna 3Keynote 3

    Power Electronics for Energy Storage Systems: Converter Topologies, Control and Integration Challenges for Grid-Connected Battery Applications

    Prof. Unai Iraola Iriondo · Mondragon Unibertsitatea, EspanhaSpain
    Resumo (em inglês)Abstract

    Prof. Unai Iraola Iriondo discusses power electronic converters as the interface between batteries and the grid. The talk reviews DC-DC and DC-AC stages, two-level, multilevel and modular topologies, and the main integration challenges: efficiency, power density and reliability, grid-following and grid-forming control, the interaction with the BMS and the effect of converter operation on battery lifetime.

    It closes with lessons from industrial applications and trends such as wide-bandgap semiconductors and reconfigurable converters.

    Palestra magna 4Keynote 4

    Modeling and Simulation of Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) in Electric Power Distribution Systems Using OpenDSS

    Caio Marco dos Santos Junqueira
    Resumo (em inglês)Abstract

    This talk shows how battery energy storage systems can be modelled and simulated in distribution networks with OpenDSS, an open-source tool that supports quasi-static time-series simulation and integration with Python.

    It focuses on the Storage element (ratings, efficiency, state of charge, operating modes and dispatch strategies) and on practical examples such as peak shaving, voltage regulation, reverse power flow mitigation, loss reduction and photovoltaic integration.

  • 15:00–16:00
    Palestra técnicaTechnical talk

    ReliaBESS: A Software Tool for the Simulation and Sizing of BESS Considering Load and Generation Profiles, Battery Aging and Economic Feasibility

    Prof. Allan Fagner Cupertino
    Resumo (em inglês)Abstract

    Prof. Allan Fagner Cupertino presents ReliaBESS, a tool that combines technical performance, battery degradation and economic analysis to size battery energy storage systems. It simulates BESS operation under realistic load and generation profiles, estimates cell aging from factors such as depth of discharge, state of charge, temperature and cycling, and translates the results into lifetime, replacement needs and economic indicators such as net present value and payback.
